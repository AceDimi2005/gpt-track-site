Frequently asked questions

The app does not show my steps or weight from Health

Open the Health app → your profile picture → Apps → GPT Track and allow reading. In GPT Track, Settings → Health must have "Read weight, steps and active energy" on. Health data is not available on iPad or in the simulator.

My meals are not in the Health app

Settings → Health → "Write food, water and weight to Health" must be on, and the Health permission sheet must allow writing for nutrition. Entries logged before you turned writing on are not written retroactively.

Does the diary sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes, through your private iCloud database, as long as both devices use the same Apple Account and iCloud is on for GPT Track (Settings app → your Apple Account → iCloud). Weigh-ins are kept out of iCloud on purpose and travel through the Health app instead. Settings → Data in the app shows the current sync state.

How do I change my calorie or macro targets?

Settings → Profile & goals. Targets are recomputed when you change your body data, activity or goal; you can also edit every number directly.

A food is wrong or missing

Search results come from Open Food Facts, a community database. You can correct a product on openfoodfacts.org, or create your own food in GPT Track (Add → My foods → New food).

What is the ChatGPT plugin and do I need it?

No, it is optional. It lets ChatGPT read and update your diary through a small server you run yourself. Setup is described in the project documentation.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Data → Delete all removes the diary from the device and from iCloud. Health samples can be removed in the Health app (Sharing → Apps → GPT Track → Delete All Data). See the privacy policy.

Something crashed or looks wrong

Email us the iOS version, the device, what you did and, if possible, a screenshot. Nothing is reported automatically, so we only learn about problems you tell us about.
